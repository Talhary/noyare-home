🌐 How the Local Tunnels Work

Noyare handles background tunnel lifecycle processes natively inside our Rust backend:

  • Process Shield Wrapper: Spawns and supervises underlying cloudflared daemon processes as background threads, guaranteeing they shut down cleanly when the app closes.
  • Local Port Intercept: Automates standard configurations to route localhost server traffic directly to standard HTTPS tunnels.
  • Zero Config DNS: Generates unique cloudflare subdomains instantly with active SSL certificates without requiring complex local environmental credentials or subscriptions.
