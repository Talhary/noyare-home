Noyare Privacy Policy

Your machine's data never leaves your machine. Full stop.

1. Zero Telemetry & Tracking

Noyare is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. The application contains no analytics libraries, no tracking scripts, and no telemetry tracking. We do not count active users, we do not log your interaction events, and we have zero interest in gathering system metrics from your environment.

2. Local-First Engineering

Everything Noyare does is calculated, processed, and displayed entirely locally.

  • All process stats, PID listings, and thread trees are retrieved using local OS kernel APIs.
  • All port listings, socket binds, and protocol statuses are gathered locally.
  • All network DNS sniffers and connection streams capture flows at the interface level, showing you live traffic on your system, without sending a single byte outbound.
  • Our cleaner checks only local known system paths (like npm caches, temp directories, or browser caches) and never accesses user documents.

3. Certificate Authority & Proxy intercept

The local HTTPS interception feature runs a strictly local reverse-proxy. When you generate a Certificate Authority (CA) inside the settings panel, the private key and root certificate are generated exclusively on your storage drive. No third party (including the developers of Noyare) can access your certs, intercept your traffic, or view captured proxy logs.

4. Verified Store Distribution

Noyare is distributed through the official Microsoft Store, ensuring that the application package is digitally signed, sandboxed, and verified by Microsoft for security and compliance.

5. Updates and Outbound Connections

Noyare does not silently dial home. Software updates are handled safely and automatically through the Microsoft Store distribution channel, keeping your installation secure without custom background updater processes.
